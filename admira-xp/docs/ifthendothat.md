# IF·THEN·DO THAT · TPV / POS

## Español

Abre /ifthendothat desde el CLI o IF·THEN·DO THAT en Avanzado. IF elige Cojo un muffin o Llevo un muffin a la caja. DO · reacción elige Música, Locución, Imagen o Vídeo. El filtro de Pixeria se preselecciona como #musica, #locucion, #imagen o #video; puedes cambiarlo por otro hashtag o una búsqueda. DO THAT · contenido permite elegir una pieza compatible del repositorio Pixeria; Abrir repositorio Pixeria abre su ficha. Cambiar reacción conserva el evento y preselecciona el filtro, pero deja el contenido pendiente de escoger. La regla se guarda al editar; seleccionar no reproduce. Arrastre, Enter/Espacio y /demo tpv ejecutan la misma regla una vez por gesto. Música y locuciones suenan en el gemelo; imágenes y vídeos aparecen temporalmente en la pantalla TPV. Detener reacción, Escape o /demo off detienen y recuperan la programación anterior; el vídeo también termina naturalmente, la imagen permanece hasta detener. Desactivar o eliminar todas las reglas no reproduce contenido. El catálogo consulta hasta 200 piezas por tipo music, audio, locucion, image y video, conserva caché y reglas anteriores de canciones (version 1). Prioridad mayor gana; en empate, la última. Las reglas se guardan en este navegador y dominio. El gemelo entra en Matrix por defecto; quality o visual explícito conserva la elección. /marca starbucks muestra el imagotipo y el texto STARBUCKS blanco; /marca off restaura la marca genérica. Sin cobro ni órdenes a TPV físico, escrituras MCP o sincronización multiusuario.

## English

Open /ifthendothat from the CLI or IF·THEN·DO THAT in Advanced. IF selects I pick up a muffin or I take a muffin to the register. DO · reaction selects Music, Voiceover, Image or Video. The Pixeria filter is preset to #musica, #locucion, #imagen or #video; you can change it to another hashtag or search. DO THAT · content selects a compatible item from the Pixeria repository; Open Pixeria repository opens its entry. Changing reaction keeps the event and presets its filter, but requires choosing content. Editing saves the rule without playing it. Drag, Enter/Space and /demo tpv execute the same rule once per gesture. Music and voiceovers play in the twin; images and videos appear temporarily on the POS display. Stop reaction, Escape or /demo off stops playback and restores the previous schedule; video also ends naturally, images remain until stopped. Disabled or deleted rules play no content. The catalogue queries up to 200 items per type music, audio, locucion, image and video, retaining cache and previous song rules (version 1). Higher priority wins; the last wins ties. Rules persist in this local browser and domain. Matrix is the entry default; explicit quality or visual links retain their choice. /brand starbucks shows the imago and white STARBUCKS wordmark; /brand off restores the generic identity. No payment or physical POS commands, MCP writes or multiuser sync.

## Contrato compartido / Shared contract

- XPL events: `muffinPicked`, `muffinDelivered`; actions `playSong`, `playVoice`, `showImage`, `playVideo` with Stock ID and optional editable `filter`. XPL.evalCondition evaluates the event.
- Scope: Matrix, `alsea-sbux-021`, `starbucks-tpv-01`; local key `xpaceos.xpl.retail.v1:alsea-sbux-021:starbucks-tpv-01`. Payload version 2 reads version 1 song rules without changing IDs, enabled states or chosen songs; legacy `xpl_rules` is retained.
- Sources: `retail-rules.mjs`, `retail-rule-composer.mjs`, `retail-media-display.mjs`, existing `xpl-composer.js` and `xpl-runtime.js`. A gesture snapshots the rule; edits apply next gesture. Selected media must match its reaction kind, independently of a custom filter.
- Repository: `https://www.admira.store/admira-xp/media-catalog`, GET only; fixed allowlist type=all|music|audio|locucion|image|video, upstream `https://api.admira.store/stock/list?type=<type>&limit=200`. Only public id/type/title/url/tags, HTTPS Stock media; no browser Cookie/Authorization forwarding. Retained music-only compatibility URL: `/admira-xp/music-catalog`.
- Default song: `1790706121644-y5mqrq` · Bad Times Deep House · Stock 1308. Música/locución suppress the existing music temporarily. Video audio does the same. Visual reaction overlays `starbucks-tpv-01` without editing any playlist, mapping or shared Matrix state; Stop/dispose removes it.
- API retained: `window.XpacePOSExperience.prepareRules()`, `pickProduct("muffin")`, `demo.start/stop/state`; events `xpace:retail-rules`, `xpace:retail-song` now include kind; songTitle remains compatible for CLI status.
- Pending / Pendiente: more products and conditions, authenticated shared rules, physical POS integration. No new remote MCP write tools.
