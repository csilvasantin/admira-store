# Starbucks · Hilo musical y TPV visual / Music and POS visual

## Español

En Starbucks, Opciones → Hilo musical → Playlist actual muestra las siete piezas del iPhone iphone17-mupi, en el mismo orden: seis manuales y una automática, sin duplicados. El aviso inicial «Playlist vacía» desaparece al llegar la fuente; los mensajes de acciones se conservan en las actualizaciones. Anterior, Siguiente y Lanzar cambian el único transporte musical virtual. Al recibir la fuente avanza sin sonido; Escuchar activa el audio sin reiniciar la pista. La pantalla vertical detrás del TPV sigue esa pista y posición, siempre sin audio propio. Los vídeos se muestran completos sobre una carátula de fondo; las dos piezas de audio usan una carátula editorial con su título. La carátula también aparece durante carga, error, fotogramas negros y los últimos dos segundos de cada vídeo. Se recupera el vídeo al llegar una imagen válida. Pausar TPV pausa sólo su imagen; al reanudar vuelve al punto del hilo. Reacciones, cesta, avisos y locuciones conservan prioridad. La lista compartida se edita en Stock → Starbucks → Hilo musical; aquí es de lectura. El iPad conserva sus dos vídeos horizontales y la cola automática.

## English

In Starbucks, Options → Background music → Current playlist shows the seven iphone17-mupi items in the same order: six manual items and one automatic item, without duplicates. The initial “Empty playlist” notice clears when the source arrives; action messages survive list refreshes. Previous, Next and Launch use the single virtual music transport. It starts muted when the source arrives; Listen enables sound without restarting the track. The portrait screen behind the POS follows that track and position, always without its own audio. Videos fit in full over a cover background; the two audio items use an editorial cover with their title. The cover also appears during loading, errors, black frames and the final two seconds of every video. Video returns when a valid picture arrives. Pause POS pauses only its picture; resuming returns to the music position. Reactions, basket, announcements and voiceovers retain priority. Edit the shared list in Stock → Starbucks → Background music; this view is read-only. The iPad retains its two landscape videos and automatic queue.

## Contrato / Contract

Source: GET https://admira.tv/api/playlist?screen=iphone17-mupi; draft.items + auto, deduplicated by stockId, physical order preserved. Proxy: https://www.admira.store/admira-xp/api/music-playlist, fixed GET only, no forwarded credentials or writes. TV timeout 6 s; optional Stock cover lookup 2 s in parallel. Client poll 15 s / timeout 8 s; waits for first valid source and retains the last valid snapshot on failure. TPV starbucks-tpv-01, existing portrait mapping/calibration preserved. starbucksMusic is the single virtual audio owner and clock; TPV video uses the exact original versioned media URL, muted and CORS-enabled. Canvas Good/Better/Best and Matrix share the visual. Editorial SVG fallback is not official album artwork. Autoplay may require Listen to retry. No physical/virtual frame sync is guaranteed; editorial seconds are preserved as metadata and browser playback follows actual media end. Legacy MCP music/tpv snapshots and static playlist JSON no longer govern the visible/playing Starbucks music/TPV. No iPhone/iPad playlist, physical player or MCP data is changed by this viewer.
