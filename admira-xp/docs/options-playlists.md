# Opciones · previos y playlists / Options · previews and playlists

## Español

Opciones muestra un previo de cada imagen, vídeo o música nueva de Stock. Generar y guardar en Stock no envían ni reproducen el contenido. Revisa el previo y pulsa Lanzar para incorporarlo y reproducirlo en las pantallas o el hilo musical del Xpacio. Añadir a playlist y arrastrar al menú Playlist actual incorporan el contenido en la posición elegida sin interrumpir la emisión actual. Los menús compactos están al final de Hilo Musical y Vídeo: el título de una fila abre el previo, ▶ salta a ese contenido, arrastrar reordena y ↑ permite ordenar con teclado. En Matrix el selector indica la playlist y sus pantallas o TPV; se conservan las asignaciones y el contenido activo. Las imágenes duran 30 segundos en la playlist. Los previos de vídeo son silenciosos y no arrancan solos; el previo musical se escucha sólo al pulsar Play y baja temporalmente el hilo. Las canciones recibidas de PixerIA esperan en el previo. Los contenidos recién creados se excluyen de la selección automática XPL. Las locuciones conservan su botón explícito Emitir y sus tres lecturas. La emisión corresponde al reproductor virtual de esta página; no confirma publicación física.

## English

Options shows a preview of each new Stock image, video or music track. Generating and saving to Stock never send or play the content. Review it and press Launch to add it and play it on the Xpace screens or background music. Add to playlist and dropping onto Current playlist insert the content at the chosen position without interrupting current playback. Compact menus appear at the bottom of Background music and Video: a row title opens its preview, ▶ jumps to that content, dragging reorders and ↑ supports keyboard ordering. In Matrix the selector identifies the playlist and its screens or POS; assignments and the active item are retained. Playlist images last 30 seconds. Video previews stay muted and never autoplay; music previews require Play and temporarily lower background music. Tracks delivered by PixerIA wait in preview. Newly generated content is excluded from automatic XPL selection. Announcements keep their explicit Play announcement action and three readings. Playback refers to this page’s virtual player; it does not confirm physical broadcasting.

## Contratos para agentes / Agent contracts

- Stock generation endpoints, identity, voice language and durable job IDs stay unchanged. Recovery never repeats payment. A successful Stock receipt is staged using `XpaceMediaOptions.stage(kind, receipt)`; it never touches a player.
- Receipt IDs and URLs are validated against `https://api.admira.store/stock/asset/<id>`. Ready previews survive a reload in this tab; generated Stock assets remain archived independently.
- `XpaceOptionsPlayback` adapts the existing Good/Better/Best music and screen lists. `XpaceMatrixOptions` adapts current Matrix playlist assignments, current track and existing owner permissions. No browser fleet key.
- Matrix changes keep a UUID playlist when owned, otherwise clone the selected group to a new owned UUID. Reads and writes use the existing `/playlists` API; device assignments stay local. Music edits use the existing local transport and do not add hardware or a paid generator.
- Shared screen tracks optionally accept `kind:"image"`. Images use a muted poster and a 30-second clock; video tracks retain their old shape and loop behavior. MCP `playlist_list`, `playlist_add`, `playlist_reorder`, `playlist_play` and `matrix_device_layout` remain available; use fresh expected revisions and the agent’s own credentials for writes.
- Newly created `admira-xp` assets remain available in Stock and explicit lookup. Automatic XPL fallback excludes them, including the local cache. An explicit Options launch takes precedence over automatic XPL on the unpinned Xpace screens; manual pins remain intact.
- Empty or failed operations never claim playback. Adding/dropping onto a playlist does not launch, a failed save retains the old schedule, and duplicate Stock references are not duplicated in the same playlist.
- Previews and playback are local. No physical broadcast acknowledgement, DNS migration or new secret/binding is included.


## Pixeria y arrastre / Pixeria and screen drops

ES: Opciones → Hilo Musical, Imagen o Vídeo conecta con la biblioteca publicada de Pixeria: elige un #hashtag y un contenido del desplegable. Importar prepara el previo sin emitir ni generar una copia. La generación está en Experto → Crear contenidos: imagen y vídeo conservan sus campos y trabajos recuperables; música abre el creador de Pixeria y sus entregas quedan preparadas en Stock. Arrastra la imagen o el asa del previo, o una fila de la playlist, directamente a la pantalla elegida en Good, Better, Best o Matrix. Soltar en una pantalla muestra sólo allí el contenido, sin audio y de forma temporal; Recargar pantalla recupera su programación guardada. En Matrix funciona con Layout cerrado y conserva las otras pantallas, TPV, asignaciones, IDs y música; las pantallas apagadas permanecen apagadas. Soltar en Playlist actual añade sin cambiar la emisión; Lanzar sigue incorporando y reproduciendo en el grupo elegido. El filtro de hashtags es exacto; también admite títulos y números de Stock. El catálogo mantiene los IDs y URLs estables y usa el archivo original disponible. La calidad de la vista no cambia la resolución del archivo. Son reproductores virtuales de este Xpacio, sin publicación física.

EN: Options → Background music, Image or Video connects to the published Pixeria library: choose a #hashtag and an item from the dropdown. Importing stages a preview without playback or generating a copy. Generation is in Expert → Create media: image and video retain their fields and recoverable jobs; music opens the Pixeria creator and its deliveries become staged Stock media. Drag the image or preview handle, or a playlist row, directly onto the chosen screen in Good, Better, Best or Matrix. Dropping on a screen displays the content only there, muted and temporarily; Reload screen restores its saved schedule. Matrix works with Layout closed and preserves other screens, POS, assignments, IDs and music; powered-off screens remain off. Dropping onto Current playlist adds without changing playback; Launch still adds and plays in the chosen group. Hashtag filters match exactly; titles and Stock numbers also work. The catalog retains stable IDs and URLs and uses the available original file. View quality does not change file resolution. These are virtual players in this Xpace, without physical publishing.

Guide: https://www.admira.store/admira-xp/docs/pixeria-screen-drop.md

## Agarre centrado / Centre grip

El punto de cogida y de destino es el centro del previo gráfico: imagen, fotograma de vídeo o carátula de audio/música. La miniatura se centra bajo el cursor, sin desplazamiento lateral ni reajuste al borde de la ventana; el destino se comprueba en ese mismo punto al pasar y soltar. Hilo Musical permite importar también archivos de audio y locuciones publicados en Stock, conservando su ID y tipo original en el catálogo. Arrastra su carátula a la playlist musical; los controles de audio quedan debajo. Imagen y vídeo van a pantallas o su playlist. Soltar en una playlist añade sin reproducir. Escape o soltar fuera cancela. Los previos de Playlist del dispositivo y las miniaturas de Signage también se cogen por el centro.

The pickup and target point is the centre of the graphic preview: image, video frame or audio/music cover. The thumbnail is centred under the cursor, without an offset or repositioning at the window edge; hover and drop use that same point. Background music also imports audio files and announcements published in Stock, keeping their ID and original catalogue type. Drag their cover to the music playlist; audio controls remain below. Images and videos go to screens or their playlist. A playlist drop adds without playback. Escape or dropping outside cancels. Device playlist previews and Signage thumbnails are also picked up at their centre.
