02The hardware that makes the demo possible

XpaceOS needs low-latency local inference so the audience captured by the camera can trigger relevant content before the visitor looks away. Lenovo edge and Nvidia GPU are the two pillars that make the booth demo viable without depending on cloud connectivity.

LENOVO · COMPUTE EDGE

ThinkEdge — the booth server

Compact, fanless in passive variants, certified for retail environments. It hosts the XpaceOS runtime and the Digital Twin, while also serving local signage when venue connectivity is unreliable.

  • ThinkEdge SE350 / SE450 depending on scenario
  • Provisioning through the ADmiraNeXT setup wizard
  • 4G/5G failover if venue Wi-Fi drops
  • Telemetry aggregated into the XpaceOS dashboard
NVIDIA · AI INFERENCE

RTX / Jetson — the inference that moves the booth

Dedicated GPU for Audience AI (anonymous profile, estimated gender/age), gesture recognition on interactive screens and PixerIA inference when a visitor requests live content from the booth.

  • Audience capture without sending images to the cloud
  • Local PixerIA for live demos
  • Digital Twin response under 200 ms
  • Multi-stream: screens + tablets + camera

04PixerIA · Creation layer

Structured brief → signage-ready asset in minutes

The retailer does not need an external agency: PixerIA generates the content inside the platform.

Four content types — Audio, Music, Images, Video — each with its own operational brief. The platform orchestrates the right engines for each case: multilingual TTS for voice, Suno with custom lyrics for sonic brand identity, Imagen 4 / FLUX for visual batches, Veo 3 for video with native audio.

SUNOchirp-v4 / v4.5 · custom lyrics · 2 clips per song
LYRIA 3Google · 30s or 2min with sung vocals
ELEVENLABSultra-realistic multilingual voices
IMAGEN 4 · FLUX · GROKstatic image 1024-2K

Each asset is delivered with a descriptive title and auto-generated cover, ready to push to the Digital Twin feed with one click.

05Admira XP · Distribution layer

Digital signage on top of a Digital Twin of the physical space

The simulator is the first demo, but also the production control tool.

Admira XP renders your store or booth in isometric perspective with real customers, screens, window display, counter and scent device. Every screen in the digital twin is a configurable real surface: assign playlists, schedule audio takes, define rules by time/weather/customer profile and see the result before installing a single device in the physical store. And when the space is live, the same twin becomes the real-time control panel.

06OmniPublicity · Marketplace layer

Real-Time Bidding on every visible physical slot

The same model as Google Ads and The Trade Desk, on your real screens.

Every time a real customer enters a screen's field of view, OmniPublicity triggers an auction in milliseconds. Advertisers bid based on context (time, weather, available stock), audience (Club profile, gender, age) and moment. The best fit wins, not the biggest budget.

RTB < 200msauction per impression, not per annual contract
OMNICHANNEL INVENTORYscreens, windows, vending, counter, PWA
BIDDING CONTEXTtime, weather, customer profile, stock, active promo
ELEGANT FALLBACKif nobody bids, retailer-owned content
TRACEABILITYeach impression signed with surface, winner, CPM, attention
SHARED BACKENDsame XpaceOS — no separate integration
